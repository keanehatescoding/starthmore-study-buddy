How the SM-2 Spaced Repetition Algorithm Works

Strathmore Study Buddy uses the proven SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) spaced repetition algorithm to fight the forgetting curve. Each quiz question tracks an individualized ease factor, interval, and repetition count:

  • First Recall: New items are scheduled for review within 1 day.
  • Expanding Intervals: Consecutive correct recalls multiply the interval (e.g. 1 day → 6 days → 15 days), requiring less study time as memories become permanent.
  • Adaptive Difficulty: Wrong answers reset the repetition counter while lowering ease, giving you extra practice where you need it most.
